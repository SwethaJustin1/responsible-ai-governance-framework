# AI Governance Framework

A practical governance framework for responsible AI deployment inside organisations.

Built and maintained by Swetha Justin — AI Strategy & Responsible AI practitioner based in Pune, India.

---

## What this is

Most organisations deploying AI have principles. Very few have mechanisms.

This framework bridges that gap. It is a structured, practical governance document covering the five things an organisation needs to deploy AI responsibly: risk classification, bias testing, transparency requirements, human oversight, and accountability. It is written for AI leaders, not lawyers — designed to be used in the room where deployment decisions are made.

---

## Who this is useful for

- AI Strategy leads building governance processes from scratch
- Responsible AI practitioners who need a structured framework to work from
- Product leaders who want governance built into development, not bolted on after
- Organisations preparing for EU AI Act compliance or India AI policy alignment

## Design principles

**1. Mechanisms, not principles.**
Principles without implementation structures are decoration. Every section of this framework specifies what to do, not just what to value.

**2. Proportionate to risk.**
Governance requirements scale with the risk level of the system. A low-risk internal tool does not need the same oversight as a high-risk credit or hiring system.

**3. India-context aware.**
This framework is designed for organisations operating in India, with references to RBI guidelines, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, and the India AI Mission where relevant. It also references EU AI Act standards for organisations with global ambitions.

**4. Built to be used, not filed.**
Every section ends with a checklist. The framework is designed to be opened in a meeting, not stored in a policy repository.
